# 2. SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & REQUIRED MODULES

## Module A: Analytical Ocean, 4-Point Buoyancy & Bilge Flooding (`OceanWaveManager` & `BuoyancyBody`)
- **Wave Math:** Deterministic stateless function returning wave height `Y` and surface normal `Vector3` at any world `(X, Z)` coordinate.
- **Multi-Probe Buoyancy:** Every ship and floating loot crate has a `Rigidbody` and an array of 4 to 6 submerged probe transforms (Bow, Stern, Port, Starboard).
- In `FixedUpdate`, compare each probe's world `Y` against `OceanWaveManager.GetWaveHeight`. If submerged (`depth > 0`), apply upward Archimedes force via `rb.AddForceAtPosition` proportional to displaced volume, plus velocity-opposing water drag and angular damping.
- **Dynamic Flooding Mass:** `BuoyancyBody` exposes a `FloodingRatio [0f .. 1f]` driven by Hull breaches. As flooding increases, `Rigidbody.mass` scales upward and buoyancy force scales down, causing a leaking ship to physically ride lower in the water and list heavily before sinking.

## Module B: Sailing, Wind & Subsystem-Driven Locomotion (`ShipLocomotionController` & `WindManager`)
- **Global Wind:** `WindManager` smoothly rotates a global wind direction vector and wind strength scalar `[0.2f .. 1.0f]`.
- **Sail State Machine:** `Anchored` (0%), `Rowing / Oars` (Starter Rowboat wind-independent propulsion), `Battle Sails` (50% speed, tight turning), and `Full Canvas` (100% speed, wind-angle multiplier).
- **Subsystem Penalties:**
  - Max thrust is multiplied by `ShipSubsystemController.SailsEfficiency [0.15f .. 1.0f]` and `CrewManager.SailHandlingEfficiency`.
  - Rudder torque is multiplied by `ShipSubsystemController.RudderEfficiency [0.1f .. 1.0f]`. If Rudder health is below 25%, inject periodic angular noise so the ship pulls unpredictably.

## Module C: Localized Ship Parts Status, Broadside Combat & Pillaging (`ShipSubsystemController`, `NavalCombatController`, `LootableWreck`)
- **4 Localized Ship Subsystems (Each `0%` to `100%` Integrity):**
  1. **Hull & Planking:** Hit by lower-deck water-line shots. Below 50% integrity, the ship springs active leaks (`LeakRate` increases `BilgeWater` per second). At 0% Hull or 100% Bilge Water, the ship enters the `Sinking` state.
  2. **Sails & Rigging:** Hit by high-elevation shots above deck level. Directly reduces top speed and acceleration. Below 20% integrity, the mast mesh visually tilts/swaps to a broken state and locks sail state out of `Full Canvas`.
  3. **Rudder & Helm:** Hit by stern-raking shots (hits within the rear 20% bounding box of the hull). Cripples turn rate.
  4. **Gun Deck (Port & Starboard Batteries):** Hit by mid-deck broadsides. Dynamically disables individual cannon barrels on that side (e.g., at 50% Gun Deck integrity, a 6-cannon battery only fires 3 cannons) and increases reload cooldown.
- **Aiming & Ballistics:**
  - Holding Port or Starboard fire projects a 3D aiming arc on the water. Dragging up/down adjusts pitch (allowing players to intentionally aim high at Sails/Rigging or low at the Hull waterline).
- **Pillaging & Floating Loot:**
  - Sinking a ship spawns 2–4 buoyant `LootCrate` objects containing Gold, Timber/Canvas (used for at-sea part repairs), Trade Goods, or Treasure Maps.

## Module D: Crew Management, Tavern Hiring & Officer Specialties (`CrewManager`, `CrewSpecialistSO`, `TavernRecruitment`)
- **General Deckhands & Manpower Efficiency:**
  - Each ship hull defines `MinRequiredCrew` and `MaxCrewCapacity` (e.g., Rowboat: 1/2; Cutter: 4/12; Brigantine: 10/30; Galleon: 25/80).
  - Broadside hits to the Gun Deck or Hull have a calculated chance to cause crew casualties.
  - If `CurrentDeckhands < MinRequiredCrew`, global reload speed, sail transition speed, and bilge pumping rate suffer a proportional manpower penalty down to `35%` efficiency.
- **5 Officer Specialty Slots (Hired at Port Taverns):**
  - Taverns generate a seeded roster of hireable Specialists (`Common`, `Veteran`, `Legendary` tiers) with a hiring cost in Gold and a passive stat modifier:
  1. **Carpenter (Hull & Repair Specialist):** Passively consumes `Timber` from cargo to repair **Hull** and **Rudder** integrity over time (`+X% HP/sec` out of combat, `0.25x` rate in combat).
  2. **Boatswain / Bosun (Bilge & Deckhand Specialist):** Operates bilge pumps to drain `BilgeWater` faster than leaks accumulate, and reduces crew casualty probability during enemy broadsides by `15%–40%`.
  3. **Master Gunner (Ordnance Specialist):** Reduces broadside reload time by `10%–35%`, tightens cannonball spread, and repairs disabled **Gun Deck** cannons between volleys.
  4. **Sailmaster (Rigging & Wind Specialist):** Passively consumes `Canvas` to mend **Sails & Rigging** at sea, and improves close-hauled wind efficiency when sailing against the wind.
  5. **Quartermaster (Loot & Economy Specialist):** Increases Gold and Repair Supplies salvaged from pillaged `LootCrates` by `15%–50%` and lowers Port Bribe and Shipyard upgrade costs.
